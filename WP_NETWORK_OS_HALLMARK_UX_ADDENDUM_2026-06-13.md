# West Peek Network OS — Hallmark UX Addendum

Status: IMPLEMENTATION BASELINE ACTIVE — BROWSER EVIDENCE STILL REQUIRED

## Brand constraints
Preserve West Peek identity, existing approved colors, logo, typography intent, and investor/operator tone. Do not replace the product with a generic neutral SaaS aesthetic.

## Required review surfaces
- Settings: Google Sheets refresh and maintenance explainers
- Deal Flow Intake and intelligent inbox review
- duplicate and concurrency feedback
- loading, empty, filtered-empty, error, and recovery states
- active/history navigation
- contact archive/restore
- event revoke/restore
- approval and notification lifecycle
- mobile navigation and keyboard operation

## Locked UX laws
- Every provider action explains what it does, what it changes, and what it never changes.
- Generic `unavailable` errors are forbidden when a diagnosable cause exists.
- Success state is not shown until persistence/readback is proven.
- Intelligent inbox classifications remain reviewable and disclose source mailbox and reasons.
- Founder inquiries may enter pending Intake; they do not become Contacts automatically.
- Brand-constrained issues are documented rather than silently restyled.

## Evidence requirement
Run the canonical Hallmark evidence collector against the final implementation, then perform expert hostile review and attach screenshots/evidence IDs before final design approval.
